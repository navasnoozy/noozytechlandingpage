# Fire & Safety ERP

What this site may say about the ERP. It's in development, so most of this page is about restraint.

## Name and labels

- It has no product name yet. Call it **Fire & Safety ERP**.
- Status label: **Fire & Safety ERP · In development**
- Describe it as **an enterprise solution for fire & safety companies**.
- When a name is chosen, update this file and [decision 0006](../decisions/0006-erp-as-unnamed-preview.md) first.

## Who it's for

Fire & safety companies that install and maintain fire protection systems: project contractors, maintenance and service companies, and suppliers. Many do all three.

## What it will cover

Planned, not built. Always write about it in the future tense ("will", "is being built"), never as something people can use today. The home page shows at most three of these; the ERP page at `/fire-safety-erp` may cover them all.

- **Projects:** from enquiry and estimate to installation, testing, commissioning and handover.
- **Maintenance contracts and service:** planned visits with checklists, breakdown calls, and service reports with photos and customer signatures.
- **Sales:** quotations with bills of quantities, costs and margins, and internal approval.
- **Scheduling:** a dispatch board for technicians and teams.
- **Stock and purchasing:** suppliers, purchase orders, and stock in stores, vans and on site.
- **Billing:** tax invoices, credit notes, receipts and statements.
- **Compliance:** equipment records, open defects and certificates for every building.
