# Registrations and legal status

How NoOzyTech's legal status and government registrations appear on the site. Researched in October 2026 from official sources, linked below. This isn't legal advice: have a lawyer or chartered accountant confirm the final wording before launch.

## What each one is

| | What it is | Issued by | What it shows | What it doesn't show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Legal entity** | A private limited company (CIN), an LLP (LLPIN), or a sole proprietorship (no registration number) | Ministry of Corporate Affairs, for companies and LLPs | That the business exists in that legal form | — |
| **DPIIT recognition** (Startup India) | A *Certificate of Recognition*, numbered "DIPP…", valid up to 10 years from incorporation | Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry | That DPIIT recognized the entity as a startup under G.S.R. 108(E), 2026 | Approval of a product, its quality, or tax benefits (a separate 80-IAC certificate) |
| **Udyam registration** (MSME) | A free, self-declared registration with a permanent number (`UDYAM-XX-00-0000000`) and a size class that's reassessed each year | Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises | That the enterprise is registered as an MSME of that size | Any certification, quality check or endorsement |
| **NSWS account** | An account on the National Single Window System, the portal used to apply for approvals, including DPIIT recognition | DPIIT; run by Invest India | Nothing on its own | — |

- A Startup India **profile**, which anyone can create, is not DPIIT **recognition**.
- A sole proprietorship can't get DPIIT recognition. Any entity type can register under Udyam.
- None of these registers a product. Never call Caps Money Manager or the ERP "government registered" or "government approved". For a finance app, that would also suggest approval from a financial regulator.

## Rules

1. **Text only.** Never show the State Emblem (the Lion Capital) or anything resembling it, the national flag or the chakra, or any ministry or programme logo: Startup India, MSME, Digital India, Make in India. Using them for business is prohibited (State Emblem Act 2005, ss.3–4; Emblems and Names Act 1950, s.3 with schedule entries 4 and 7). The Startup India logo needs DPIIT's written approval, and recognition doesn't grant it.
2. **No certificate images or PDFs.** The DPIIT certificate carries the State Emblem. The Udyam certificate shows the owner's personal details, including social category.
3. **Say "recognized" or "registered".** Never "approved", "certified", "endorsed", "authorized", "partner of" or "backed by" the Government. Claiming a sponsorship, approval or affiliation you don't have is an unfair trade practice (Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.2(47)), and a website counts as an advertisement (s.2(1)). A disclaimer can't fix a misleading badge (CCPA guidelines 2022, g.11).
4. **Make every claim checkable.** Give the number, and link it to the official verification page.
5. **Keep it current.** Show DPIIT's valid-until date. Show the Udyam size class with its classification year. Update or remove either one the day it changes.
6. **Credibility supports, it doesn't lead.** These lines belong in the footer and on the About page, never in a hero, and never as badges or seals.

## Wording

Use only the lines that match the documents NoOzyTech actually holds ([company facts](../product/company.md#registrations)).

**Legal entity**, on the About page and in the footer:

| Legal form | Wording |
|---|---|
| Private limited company | NoOzyTech Private Limited · CIN U… · Registered office: … |
| LLP | NoOzyTech LLP, a limited liability partnership registered under the LLP Act, 2008 · LLPIN … · Registered office: … |
| Sole proprietorship | NoOzyTech is a sole proprietorship owned by Navas CK, based in [city], India. |

**DPIIT recognition**, only with a Certificate of Recognition:

- Long, on the About page: "Recognized as a startup by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Government of India, under the Startup India initiative. Certificate No. DIPP…, valid until [date]. [Verify on Startup India]"
- Short, in the footer and the founder line: "DPIIT-recognized startup"

**Udyam registration**:

- Long, on the About page: "Registered under Udyam Registration (Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) as a [micro] enterprise, classification year [2026–27]. Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-…. [Verify on the Udyam portal]"
- Short, in the footer: "Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-…"

**NSWS**: not mentioned. It's how the DPIIT application was filed, not a status.

## Placement

- **Footer, every page:** one fine-print line with the legal name (and CIN or LLPIN), "DPIIT-recognized startup", and the Udyam number. For a private limited company, the Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, r.26, require the home page to show the name, registered office address, CIN, phone, email, and a named contact for queries and grievances. Putting these in the footer covers that requirement.
- **About page, "Company details":** the full list, with the long wordings and verification links.
- **Home page, founder section:** at most one plain sentence, for example "NoOzyTech is a DPIIT-recognized startup, founded in India in 2026 by Navas CK."
- **Structured data:** `legalName`, and an `identifier` entry for each registration number shown on the page.

## Upkeep

- **Udyam:** after each income tax return season, check the certificate's newest classification row, and update the size class and year on the site.
- **DPIIT:** remove the recognition lines on the valid-until date, or as soon as turnover passes ₹200 crore in a year, or if NoOzyTech becomes a subsidiary.
- **Any change of legal form** (for example a proprietorship becoming a private limited company): update the facts first, then every line above.

## Phrases to avoid

"Government approved", "Govt. certified", "Government of India recognized company", "DPIIT certified", "Startup India certified", "Startup India registered" (for a profile only), "MSME certified", "MSME-approved", "NSWS registered", "official partner of", "in association with", "backed by the Government", "a Make in India company", "a Digital India company", "tax-exempt startup" (without the 80-IAC certificate), and ® on any name that isn't a registered trademark.
