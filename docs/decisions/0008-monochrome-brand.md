# 0008: The brand is monochrome, with an oO symbol

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

NoOzyTech had no logo. The owner asked for one designed the way Apple would design it: simple, memorable, and clear at every size. The products already have their own colors. Caps uses coral, yellow and blue, and the ERP's interface uses a deep green.

## Decision

- **Wordmark:** "NoOzyTech" in Inter (Display, SemiBold, −0.02em), converted to outlines.
- **Symbol:** the small o and big O from the name, as two rings on one baseline.
- **No brand accent color.** The brand is monochrome: near-black on light, near-white on dark.
- **Interface:** also monochrome, with dark pill buttons and underlined links. Blue appears only as the keyboard focus ring.

## Options considered

- **An "N" monogram:** common, and it says nothing about the name.
- **Inter's own o and O letters as the symbol:** they read as typed text, not as a mark.
- **A brand accent color:** it would compete with the products' colors. A purple or blue gradient brand is also the most common "AI look".

## Consequences

- The products bring the color to every page, which keeps them the hero.
- The logo matches the site's typography exactly, because it's made from the same font.
- One script generates every asset, so the logo can be refined later without redrawing anything.
